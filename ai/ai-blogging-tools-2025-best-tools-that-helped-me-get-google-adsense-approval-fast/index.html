AI Blogging Tools 2025 — Best Tools That Helped Get Google AdSense Approval Fast

The exact AI blogging tools used to get Google AdSense approved and grow a WordPress site in 2025 — writing, keyword research, SEO, and image tools, all in one workflow.

D
By Deve June 4, 2025 · 8 min read
Share

AI blogging tools are changing the game for content creators in 2025. Whether you're starting a new blog or optimizing an existing one for Google AdSense approval, the right AI tools can save you time, improve content quality, and fast-track your growth.

In this guide, I'll share the exact AI blogging tools I used to get approved for Google AdSense and grow my WordPress site — all in 2025.

Why AI Tools Matter for Bloggers in 2025

In 2025, blogging is more competitive than ever. The best way to stay ahead is to use AI tools to speed up content creation, discover profitable keywords, optimize for SEO, and repurpose content across platforms.

Most importantly — if you're aiming for Google AdSense approval, these tools help ensure your content is unique, valuable, fast-loading, and consistent. Let's dive in.

Best AI Tools for Writing Blog Content (and Saving Hours)

1. ChatGPT-4.5 / GPT-4 Turbo

Best for long-form blog writing, outlines, intros, and FAQs. I generate first drafts and then polish them manually — fast, human-sounding content without fluff is exactly what helps with AdSense approval.

Pro tip: use prompts like "Write a blog post introduction on [topic] optimized for AdSense approval."

2. Jasper AI

Best for blog post generation and marketing copy, with built-in templates for blog intros, conclusions, and product reviews.

3. Notion AI

Great for quick research summaries and idea generation — a nice bonus since it's built right into your note-taking tool.

AI Tools for Keyword Research (High CPC)

4. Surfer SEO

Helps you write keyword-optimized content, with a focus on high-CPC keywords for blogging, tech, and finance niches.

5. LowFruits.io

Perfect for finding low-competition, high-intent keywords. It also shows where small blogs already rank, which is great for beginners.

6. Google Trends + Glimpse AI

Track rising blog topics, and combine with Google Search Console to spot real opportunities early.

AI Tools for SEO Optimization

7. Rank Math + Content AI

Rank Math Pro gives real-time SEO scores and content suggestions, while Content AI suggests keywords and internal links. Being schema-ready also tends to mean faster AdSense approval.

8. Frase.io

Frase.io specializes in competitive content analysis, helping plan and write blog posts optimized to outrank competitors on Google.

Key benefits: automatic SERP analysis of top-ranking pages to extract headings and key topics, smart outlining built from real search-result data, an FAQ builder pulling common questions from "People Also Ask" and forums, and a topic-scoring feature that shows how well your content covers a subject compared to the competition.

How to use it: run Frase before writing any post to identify keyword gaps and outline content with data-backed precision, add schema-optimized FAQs to increase visibility in rich results, and pair it with Rank Math's suggestions to improve on-page SEO.

AI for Blog Images, Infographics & Video Content

9. Canva Pro + Magic Design AI

Create Pinterest pins, blog banners, and YouTube thumbnails, with text overlays for SEO image ranking.

10. Pictory AI

Turns blog posts into YouTube videos in minutes — excellent for repurposing AdSense-optimized articles.

11. DALL·E 3 (Built into ChatGPT)

Use DALL·E 3 to generate unique, high-quality blog images tailored to each article topic. These AI-generated visuals are original enough to pass Google's image originality checks, making them ideal for SEO and branding.

Best practices: create a custom header image for every post that visually represents the topic, use descriptive alt text with target keywords, optimize image file names using the post's focus keyword, and compress images with a tool like TinyPNG or ShortPixel for fast load times.

How to Use These Tools Together — The Full Workflow

Here's how the full blog post workflow comes together, start to finish:

  • Use Glimpse/Trends to find a trending topic.
  • Use LowFruits + Surfer SEO to validate keywords.
  • Use ChatGPT to write an optimized outline and draft.
  • Use Canva / DALL·E to make blog images.
  • Use Rank Math to polish SEO.
  • Use Pictory to repurpose the post into YouTube Shorts.
  • Schedule social media pins via Canva.

Bonus Tips for 2025 Bloggers

  • Target high-CPC topics like AI, tech tools, personal finance, and reviews.
  • Write for humans first, optimize for SEO second.
  • Keep paragraphs short for mobile readers.
  • Use internal links to pass link value to important pages.
  • Link to authority sites (Google, Forbes, Moz, etc.) — it improves trust.
