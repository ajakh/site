The Ultimate Guide to Affordable TVs in 2024

In the market for a new TV but sticking to a tight budget? Here's your essential rundown on what features to prioritize in a cost-effective television, along with our top recommendations of models that deliver exceptional performance for under $1,000.

We consider TVs under $1,000 for a 65-inch screen as budget-friendly. Check out our top picks.

Hisense U6K

Why We Picked It

The Hisense U6K boasts impressive color reproduction, covering nearly the entire DCI-P3 digital cinema color space with remarkable accuracy straight out of the box. Its Google TV interface offers built-in far-field microphones for hands-free Google Assistant, plus support for Apple AirPlay 2 and Google Cast. It falls short in brightness compared to some competitors, but the compromises it entails are among the most reasonable we've encountered at this price.

Ideal For

If you want abundant features and relatively solid picture quality without paying over $1,000 for a 65-inch TV, the Hisense U6K is an excellent option — especially for households with both Android and iPhone users.

Amazon Fire TV Omni QLED

Why We Picked It

The Fire TV Omni QLED presents a considerable improvement over Amazon's initial Fire TV Omni, with commendable color performance and Fire TV features including hands-free Alexa and support for Apple AirPlay — all at an affordable price.

Ideal For

Budget-conscious Amazon users, particularly those who already own Echo smart speakers. It functions like a Fire TV Cube built into the screen, at a lower cost.

TCL Q7 Class TV

Why We Picked It

The Q7 Class fits perfectly into the sweet spot with impressive contrast and color. It offers more brightness than the Hisense U6K, and is available in larger sizes up to 85 inches. It doesn't quite match TCL's legendary 6-Series in brightness, but it's a solid step up from the most budget-friendly options.

Ideal For

Those who want a slight upgrade from the Hisense model but aren't looking to spend on TVs exceeding 1,000 nits of brightness. Excellent value overall.

Vizio 50-Inch M-Series Quantum X TV

Why We Picked It

One of the finest budget-friendly gaming TVs on the market. It features a 120Hz panel with variable refresh rate (VRR) and AMD FreeSync Premium for smooth gameplay, impressively low input lag, and a remarkably wide color spectrum. It uses Vizio's SmartCast platform with Apple AirPlay and Google Cast support. Its primary drawback is modest contrast and dim black levels.

Ideal For

Budget-conscious gamers seeking a sizable monitor or mid-sized TV. The 120Hz panel and FreeSync Premium are rare in budget TVs and provide a genuine competitive gaming advantage.

Amazon Fire TV 2-Series (40-Inch)

Why We Picked It

At 40 inches with a 1080p display, this TV remains crisp and vibrant despite not being 4K — and 4K matters less on smaller screens. Its affordability sets it apart, costing significantly less than other models on this list. Trade-offs include being the dimmest pick and lacking hands-free Alexa functionality.

Ideal For

Anyone looking to minimize expenses for a secondary space like a garage or spare room where a basic screen suffices. Not recommended as the primary household TV, but a cost-effective option for secondary use.

If you're on the hunt for budget-friendly TV options, this list has you covered. Whether you're after cheap TVs, affordable television models, or budget-friendly options, these top picks ensure you get the most bang for your buck.
