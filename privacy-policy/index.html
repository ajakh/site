Privacy Policy

IT Charging is committed to protecting the privacy of our users. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit IT Charging.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By accessing or using the Site, you agree to be bound by the terms and conditions described herein and accept the practices outlined in this Privacy Policy.

Collection of Your Information

We may collect information about you in a variety of ways. The information we may collect includes:

  • Personal Data: personally identifiable information, such as your name, email address, and phone number, that you voluntarily provide to us when you register with the Site or participate in various activities related to the Site.
  • Usage Data: information on how you interact with the Site, such as your IP address, browser type, the pages you visit, the links you click, and other usage data collected through cookies and similar tracking technologies.
  • Location Information: your location information if you enable location-based services on your device for the Site.
  • Payment Data: data necessary to process payment if you make purchases through the Site, such as your payment instrument number and associated security code.

We may use the information we collect for various purposes, including to:

  • Develop new products, services, features, and functionality;
  • Communicate with you to provide updates and other information relating to the Site;
  • Respond to your comments, questions, and requests, and provide customer service;
  • Monitor and analyze trends, usage, and activities in connection with our Site; and
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraudulent transactions and other illegal activities and protect the rights and property of IT Charging.

Disclosure of Your Information

We may share your information with third parties who provide services on our behalf or who assist us in operating our Site, conducting our business, or servicing you, such as payment processors, hosting providers, and analytics providers.

Security of Your Information

We take reasonable measures to protect the information we collect from unauthorized access, use, and disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
