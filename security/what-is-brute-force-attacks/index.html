What Is a Brute Force Attack?

A brute force attack is when a hacker tries lots of different passwords and usernames until they find the right ones. They use computers to quickly try out loads of combinations until they get it right.

Types of Brute Force Attacks

Basic Brute Force

The hacker tries to guess login details without special software — common passwords like password123 or simple PIN codes.

Dictionary Attack

The attacker goes through lists of words, sometimes adding numbers or symbols, to guess the password.

Hybrid Attack

Combines dictionary attacks with brute force — finding passwords like SanDiego123 by combining common words with numbers.

Credential Stuffing

Hackers use stolen username and password pairs and try them on different websites to break into accounts.

How to Prevent Brute Force Attacks

  • Use passwords longer than 10 characters with mixed characters
  • Never reuse passwords across sites
  • Enable multi-factor authentication (MFA)
  • Use a password manager
  • Enable login attempt limits on your site
